# TechConnect - 30 Second Promotional Video Script

## Total Duration: 30 seconds

---

## Scene 1 (0-5 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "Struggling to keep up with your studies?"

**Visual:**
- Student looking stressed at laptop with books piled up
- Dark/moody lighting
- Show frustrated expression
- Text overlay: "Feeling overwhelmed?"

**Animation:** Zoom in slowly on stressed student

---

## Scene 2 (5-10 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "Meet TechConnect - your personal tutoring platform."

**Visual:**
- Bright transition to TechConnect logo animation
- Clean, modern interface mockup
- Show the landing page with "Why Choose TechConnect?" section
- Text overlay: "TechConnect"

**Animation:** Logo appears with smooth fade-in, interface slides in from right

---

## Scene 3 (10-15 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "Connect with expert tutors instantly."

**Visual:**
- Split screen showing:
  - Left: Learner browsing tutor profiles
  - Right: Video call session in progress with whiteboard
- Show interactive whiteboard with math equations
- Text overlay: "Live Video Sessions • Interactive Whiteboard"

**Animation:** Quick cuts between browsing and active session

---

## Scene 4 (15-22 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "Book scheduled sessions or get instant help when you need it."

**Visual:**
- Calendar interface showing available time slots
- "Instant Session" button being clicked
- Notification animation showing tutor accepting request
- Show chat messages and file sharing
- Text overlay: "Flexible Scheduling • Instant Help"

**Animation:** Fast-paced montage of features

---

## Scene 5 (22-28 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "Real-time collaboration. Real results."

**Visual:**
- Happy student giving thumbs up after session
- 5-star rating animation
- Show dashboard with session history and progress
- Multiple students using the platform (diverse group)
- Text overlay: "Join 1000+ Students"

**Animation:** Success metrics counting up, stars filling in

---

## Scene 6 (28-30 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "TechConnect. Learn smarter, not harder."

**Visual:**
- TechConnect logo centered
- Website URL appears below: techconnect-sand.vercel.app
- Call-to-action button: "Get Started Free"
- Background: Gradient animation (blue to green)

**Animation:** Logo pulses, CTA button bounces

---

## Music Suggestion:
- Upbeat, modern electronic music
- Builds energy from calm to exciting
- Royalty-free options: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or YouTube Audio Library

---

## Color Palette:
- Primary: #10b981 (Green)
- Secondary: #3b82f6 (Blue)
- Accent: #6366f1 (Purple)
- Background: Dark mode with white text

---

## Text Overlays Style:
- Font: Modern sans-serif (Poppins, Inter, or Montserrat)
- Bold for main text
- Smooth fade-in/fade-out transitions
- Keep text on screen for 2-3 seconds minimum

---

## Tools You Can Use:

### AI Video Generators:
1. **Canva** (easiest) - canva.com
   - Use "Video" templates
   - Add AI voiceover with "Magic Media"
   - Drag and drop interface

2. **Pictory.ai** - pictory.ai
   - Paste script, it generates video automatically
   - AI voiceover included

3. **InVideo** - invideo.io
   - Text-to-video AI
   - Large template library

4. **Synthesia** - synthesia.io
   - AI avatar presenter option
   - Professional look

### For Screenshots/Mockups:
- Take screenshots of your actual TechConnect app
- Use Figma or Canva to create mockups
- Screen record your app in action

### For AI Voiceover:
- **ElevenLabs** - elevenlabs.io (most natural)
- **Murf.ai** - murf.ai
- **Play.ht** - play.ht
- **Canva** (built-in AI voice)

---

## Pro Tips:
1. Use your actual app screenshots for authenticity
2. Add subtle background music (not too loud)
3. Keep transitions smooth (0.3-0.5 seconds)
4. Use motion graphics for statistics/numbers
5. End with a clear call-to-action
6. Export in 1080p (1920x1080) or 4K
7. Add captions for accessibility and silent viewing
