# Figure Descriptions (Following Your Order)

### Figure X. Platform Landing Page
The landing page serves as the entry point to TechConnect, providing an overview of the platform's peer tutoring capabilities and features. It presents information about the system's purpose, key functionalities, and benefits for both tutors and learners. The page includes clear call-to-action buttons directing users to sign in or register based on their intended role within the platform.

### Figure X. User Authentication Screen (Login/Signup)
The authentication screen provides two distinct sign-in pathways: a standard email-password authentication for tutors and learners, and a separate OAuth-based authentication for administrators. The dual authentication approach ensures that only authorized personnel can access administrative functions. The admin authentication uses OAuth to provide enhanced security and can be configured for super admin capabilities when multiple administrative users are required in future system iterations.

### Figure X. Role Selection Interface
The role selection interface allows new users to choose between registering as a tutor or learner based on their intended platform usage. This page appears after initial account creation and directs users to the appropriate registration form. The clear role differentiation ensures that users complete the correct onboarding process and gain access to role-specific features and dashboards.

### Figure X. User Registration Forms (Tutor & Learner)
The registration forms collect essential information from both tutors and learners, with each form tailored to the specific role's requirements. While both forms share common fields such as personal information and subject interests, they differ in purpose and validation requirements. For tutors, the subject field represents their areas of expertise, while for learners it indicates their learning interests. The tutor registration form includes a required biography field that administrators use to validate qualifications and determine fitness for providing tutoring services before approval.

### Figure X. Learner Dashboard Overview
The learner dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the user's tutoring activities and platform resources. It displays upcoming sessions with scheduled tutors, completed session history, available learning resources, and recent announcements from administrators. Quick action buttons enable learners to efficiently access key features such as finding tutors, booking sessions, and viewing resources. The dashboard serves as the central hub for learners to manage their academic support activities.

### Figure X. Tutor Discovery and Search (Find Tutors)
The tutor discovery interface enables learners to browse and search for available tutors using multiple filtering criteria. The page features fuzzy search functionality powered by Levenshtein distance algorithm for intelligent name and subject matching, along with filters for online status, subject expertise, rating, and year level. Search results are ranked by relevance using rule-based scoring, helping learners find the most suitable tutors for their academic needs. Each tutor card displays key information including expertise, availability, rating, and online status.

### Figure X. Tutor Profile View
The tutor profile view displays comprehensive information about a selected tutor, accessible by clicking on a tutor card in the find tutors page. The profile includes the tutor's full name, profile picture, subject expertise, biography, availability schedule, average rating, total completed sessions, and feedback from previous learners. This detailed view helps learners make informed decisions when selecting a tutor by providing transparency about the tutor's qualifications, experience, and teaching effectiveness.

### Figure X. Favorite Tutors List
The favorite tutors feature allows learners to bookmark preferred tutors for quick access in future sessions. Learners can add tutors to their favorites list from the tutor profile page, creating a personalized collection of trusted mentors. This feature streamlines the tutor selection process for learners who have established successful learning relationships with specific tutors and wish to continue working with them.

### Figure X. Session Booking Modal
The session booking modal enables learners to schedule tutoring sessions with their selected tutor. The interface includes a calendar view displaying the tutor's available dates, a time selector showing the tutor's available time slots, and a duration selector. The system intelligently calculates available durations based on the selected start time and the tutor's availability window. For example, if a tutor is available from 9:00 AM to 9:30 AM, learners can only book a 30-minute session starting at 9:00 AM. If a tutor is available from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, learners can select any start time in 30-minute increments and choose a duration from 10 minutes up to the remaining available time. The minimum session duration is 10 minutes, with flexible increments allowing learners to customize session length based on their needs.

### Figure X. My Sessions Management (Learner)
The my sessions page displays a comprehensive list of all learner sessions organized by status: pending (awaiting tutor approval), upcoming (confirmed future sessions), completed (finished sessions), missed (sessions not attended), and cancelled (sessions terminated by either party). For completed sessions, learners have the option to leave feedback if they were disconnected during the session or forgot to provide feedback immediately after completion. This optional feedback mechanism ensures learners can share their experience even after the session has ended.

### Figure X. Session Cancellation/Rejection Modal
The cancellation modal appears when either a learner or tutor needs to cancel or reject a session. The interface requires users to select a reason for the cancellation from predefined options or provide a custom explanation. This mandatory reason helps maintain transparency and accountability within the platform. The system automatically notifies the other party about the cancellation along with the provided reason, ensuring clear communication and allowing users to understand why sessions are cancelled.

### Figure X. Learning Resource Repository (Learner View)
The learning resource repository serves as a centralized hub for all approved educational materials shared by tutors. Learners can browse, search, and filter resources by subject, tutor, or upload date. Each resource card displays the title, description, subject category, uploader information, and upload date. Resources shown in this repository are publicly available materials that tutors have chosen to share with the entire platform community. Private resources shared during individual sessions are only accessible to the specific learner and tutor involved in that session.

### Figure X. Resource Preview Interface
The resource preview interface allows learners to view learning materials before downloading them. This preview functionality helps learners determine if the resource meets their needs and is relevant to their learning objectives. The preview displays the resource content in a modal or dedicated view, with options to download the file for offline access or return to the resource repository to continue browsing.

### Figure X. Learner Profile Management
The learner profile management page allows users to view and edit their personal information, academic details, and account settings. Learners can update their full name, contact information, course, year level, subjects of interest, and profile picture. The interface provides a user-friendly form with validation to ensure data accuracy. Changes are saved to the database and immediately reflected across the platform wherever the learner's information is displayed.

### Figure X. Tutor Dashboard Overview
The tutor dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of tutoring activities and performance metrics. It displays total sessions conducted, completed sessions count, average rating from learner feedback (calculated by summing all star ratings and dividing by total feedback submissions), pending session requests awaiting approval, recent session history, and quick action buttons for common tasks. The dashboard includes an online status toggle that tutors can use to indicate their availability for instant sessions. When toggled on, learners can see the tutor is online and request immediate tutoring assistance.

### Figure X. Tutor Session Management (My Sessions - Tutor)
The tutor session management page displays all tutoring sessions organized by status, similar to the learner view but with tutor-specific actions. Tutors can approve or decline pending session requests, view upcoming confirmed sessions, cancel scheduled sessions with required reasoning, and review completed session history. The interface provides clear action buttons for each session status, enabling efficient session workflow management.

### Figure X. My Learners Directory
The my learners page displays a comprehensive list of all students who have had tutoring sessions with the tutor. Each learner entry shows their name, profile picture, course, year level, total sessions completed together, and average rating given by that learner. Tutors can click on individual learner entries to view detailed information including session history, topics covered, and feedback received. This feature helps tutors track their teaching relationships and understand their impact on individual learners.

### Figure X. Availability Management
The availability management interface allows tutors to create and modify their flexible tutoring schedule. Tutors can choose between two scheduling approaches: weekly recurring availability, which sets the same time slots for all days of the week, or day-specific availability, which allows customization for individual days. The calendar interface displays the tutor's availability with visual indicators for available and unavailable time slots. By default, all time slots in the learner's calendar view are marked as unavailable until the tutor explicitly sets their available hours. This granular control ensures tutors can balance their tutoring commitments with other academic and personal responsibilities.

### Figure X. Learner Feedback Dashboard
The learner feedback dashboard displays all ratings, comments, and tagged feedback that tutors have received from their students. Feedback is organized chronologically and includes the learner's name, session date, star rating (1-5 scale), selected feedback tags from the eleven predefined categories (Clear Explanations, Patient & Friendly, Well Prepared, Helpful Materials, Great Communication, Always On Time, Very Knowledgeable, Helped Me Improve, Engaging Session, Good Examples, Responsive), and optional written comments. This comprehensive feedback view helps tutors identify their strengths and areas for improvement, supporting continuous development of their teaching skills.

### Figure X. Resource Upload Interface
The resource upload interface enables tutors to share learning materials with the platform community. Tutors can upload files, provide a title and description, select relevant subject categories, and submit resources for administrative approval. All uploaded resources undergo moderation by administrators to ensure content quality and appropriateness before becoming publicly available in the learning resource repository. This approval workflow maintains platform standards and protects learners from inappropriate or low-quality materials.

### Figure X. Tutor Profile Management
The tutor profile management page allows tutors to view and edit their personal information, academic credentials, and platform-specific settings. In addition to standard profile fields available to learners, tutors have the option to upload a QR code for voluntary donations. Tutors can upload QR codes from payment platforms such as GCash, PayMaya, or bank transfer services. When set, this QR code is displayed to learners after session completion and feedback submission, allowing learners to optionally show appreciation through financial contributions. If no QR code is uploaded, the donation step is skipped, and the session ends immediately after feedback.

### Figure X. Administrative Dashboard Overview
The administrative dashboard provides platform-wide oversight and management capabilities. It displays key metrics including total registered users, pending tutor approvals, currently active sessions, total sessions completed, and quick action buttons for common administrative tasks. The dashboard includes a platform health indicator that monitors system performance metrics including user engagement levels (active users over time), tutor coverage (ratio of tutors to learners), and overall platform stability. This centralized view enables administrators to quickly assess platform status and identify areas requiring attention.

### Figure X. Platform Analytics
The platform analytics page provides detailed insights into TechConnect's usage patterns and performance metrics. Key statistics include total registered users, number of approved tutors, total sessions conducted, resources uploaded and approved, and session completion rate (calculated as completed sessions divided by total scheduled sessions, expressed as a percentage). The page features platform health metrics tracking user engagement, tutor coverage, and platform growth indicators showing steady, increasing, or decreasing trends based on user registration and session activity patterns over time. These analytics help administrators make data-driven decisions about platform improvements and resource allocation.

### Figure X. User Management Interface
The user management interface displays a comprehensive list of all registered platform users across all roles (tutors, learners, and administrators). The page includes search and filter capabilities to locate specific users or groups. Currently, the primary administrative action available is account deactivation, which prevents users from accessing the platform while preserving their data for potential reactivation. Future enhancements may include additional user management capabilities such as role modification, password reset assistance, and detailed activity logs.

### Figure X. Tutor Verification and Approval
The tutor approval interface enables administrators to review and validate tutor applications before granting full platform access. When users register as tutors, their accounts are created and they can log in, but all tutoring functionalities remain disabled until administrative approval. Administrators review the tutor's profile information, biography, subject expertise, and qualifications to determine fitness for providing tutoring services. Administrators can approve tutors (enabling full functionality), reject applications with a reason, or request additional information. Rejected tutors can contact administrators or visit the office for reconsideration, after which administrators can re-review and potentially approve the application.

### Figure X. Session Oversight Interface (Session Management)
The session management interface provides administrators with a comprehensive view of all tutoring sessions conducted on the platform, organized by status (pending, upcoming, completed, cancelled, missed). The primary action available is viewing detailed session logs, which opens the session logs page displaying information recorded by both tutor and learner at session completion. This oversight capability supports quality assurance, dispute resolution, and platform improvement initiatives.

### Figure X. Session Audit Logs
The session logs page displays detailed records of completed tutoring sessions for administrative review and audit purposes. Each log entry includes information filled out by both the tutor and learner at session end, such as topics covered, learning objectives achieved, challenges encountered, and session duration. These logs provide transparency and accountability, support quality monitoring, and help administrators identify patterns or issues requiring intervention.

### Figure X. Learning Resource Moderation (Resource Approval)
The resource approval interface displays all learning materials uploaded by tutors awaiting administrative review. Administrators can preview each resource to assess its quality, relevance, and appropriateness before approving it for public access in the learning resource repository. The preview functionality allows administrators to view the content without downloading, ensuring efficient moderation. Administrators can approve resources (making them publicly available), reject them with feedback to the tutor, or request revisions before final approval.

### Figure X. Announcement Creation Interface
The announcement creation interface allows administrators to compose and publish platform-wide messages to all users. The interface includes fields for announcement title and content with basic markdown formatting support. Once published, announcements appear on both tutor and learner dashboards, ensuring important information reaches the entire platform community. The straightforward interface enables quick communication of updates, policy changes, maintenance schedules, or other important platform information.

### Figure X. Real-Time Session Monitoring (Live Monitoring Page)
The live monitoring page displays all currently active tutoring sessions in real-time, providing administrators with oversight capabilities for quality assurance purposes. When sessions are in progress, they appear on this dashboard with information about the participants, session duration, and status. Administrators can join any active session at any time to observe the tutoring interaction, though this capability is intended for situations requiring intervention or quality monitoring rather than routine surveillance.

### Figure X. Administrative Session Join Notification
When an administrator joins an active tutoring session through the live monitoring feature, both the tutor and learner receive an immediate notification alerting them to the administrative presence. This transparency ensures users are aware of who is present in the session and maintains trust in the platform's oversight processes. The notification appears as a banner or modal within the session interface, clearly identifying the administrator who has joined.

### Figure X. Instant Session Request
The instant session feature enables learners to request immediate tutoring assistance when tutors are online. Instant sessions have a duration limit of 10-60 minutes, designed for quick questions or brief explanations. When a learner requests an instant session, the online tutor receives a real-time notification and can choose to admit or decline the request. Tutors control their availability for instant sessions using the online status toggle in their dashboard, which signals to learners that the tutor is available for immediate assistance.

### Figure X. Device Testing Interface
The device testing page appears before users enter a tutoring session, ensuring their camera and microphone are properly configured. The interface displays a preview of the user's video feed and audio level indicators, allowing them to verify their devices are working correctly. Users can select different camera and microphone devices if multiple options are available, adjust settings, and test audio levels by speaking. This pre-session check prevents technical issues during tutoring sessions and ensures smooth communication between tutors and learners.

### Figure X. Tutor Waiting Room
After completing device testing, tutors enter a waiting room before the session officially begins. This interface displays session information including the learner's name, scheduled time, and session topic. The waiting room provides tutors with a moment to prepare materials and mentally transition into teaching mode. Once the learner is ready to join, the tutor receives a notification and can admit them to begin the session.

### Figure X. Learner Admission Control
When a learner completes device testing and is ready to join the session, the tutor sees an admission control interface. This page displays the learner's information and provides options to admit them into the session or decline entry if there are issues. The admission control ensures tutors have final authority over when sessions begin and prevents learners from entering before tutors are prepared. Once admitted, both users enter the main session interface with full access to video, whiteboard, chat, and resource sharing features.

### Figure X. Video Session Interface
The video session interface provides a comprehensive environment for peer-to-peer tutoring with real-time collaboration tools. The main area displays video feeds from both tutor and learner using WebRTC peer-to-peer technology, eliminating the need for centralized video servers. The interface includes three key collaboration features: an interactive whiteboard using Canvas API for visual explanations and problem-solving, a real-time chat for text communication and sharing links, and a private assets section where tutors can upload session-specific resources accessible only to the current learner. Control buttons allow users to toggle camera and microphone, share screens, access settings, and end the session.

### Figure X. Session Log Entry Form
When a tutoring session ends, both the tutor and learner are prompted to complete a session log for auditing and quality assurance purposes. The log form includes fields for topics covered, learning objectives achieved, challenges encountered, and any additional notes about the session. Both users must complete their respective logs before proceeding to the next step. These logs are stored in the database and accessible to administrators through the session management interface, supporting platform oversight and continuous improvement initiatives.

### Figure X. Learner Feedback Submission
After completing the session log, learners are directed to a feedback form where they provide ratings and comments about their tutoring experience. The form includes a 5-star rating scale, eleven predefined feedback tags (Clear Explanations, Patient & Friendly, Well Prepared, Helpful Materials, Great Communication, Always On Time, Very Knowledgeable, Helped Me Improve, Engaging Session, Good Examples, Responsive), and an optional text field for additional comments. This structured feedback helps tutors understand their strengths and areas for improvement while providing future learners with insights when selecting tutors.

### Figure X. Voluntary Donation QR Code
After submitting feedback, learners see the tutor's donation QR code if the tutor has uploaded one in their profile settings. This optional feature allows learners to show appreciation for excellent tutoring through voluntary financial contributions via mobile payment platforms such as GCash, PayMaya, or bank transfers. Learners can scan the QR code with their mobile banking app to send a donation directly to the tutor. If the tutor has not uploaded a QR code, this step is skipped entirely, and the session ends immediately after feedback submission. The platform does not process payments directly; all transactions occur outside TechConnect through the learner's chosen payment method.

### Figure X. Disconnection Handling
The platform includes automatic disconnection detection to handle network issues during tutoring sessions. When one user disconnects unexpectedly, the system detects the loss of connection and starts a 5-minute countdown timer. The remaining connected user sees a notification indicating the other party has disconnected and the time remaining for reconnection. If the disconnected user rejoins within 5 minutes, the session resumes normally. If the timer expires without reconnection, the system automatically ends the session and prompts both users to complete session logs. If both users disconnect simultaneously, the same 5-minute timer applies, and the session ends automatically if neither user reconnects in time.

### Figure X. Session End Warning
As tutoring sessions approach their scheduled end time, both the tutor and learner receive a pop-up alert notification indicating the session will end soon. This warning appears 5 minutes before the scheduled end time, giving the tutor an opportunity to wrap up the current topic, summarize key points, and ensure the learner's questions are addressed. The warning helps prevent abrupt session endings and ensures both parties can conclude the session professionally.

### Figure X. Password Reset Flow
The password reset functionality allows users to securely recover access to their accounts. Users initiate the process by clicking "Forgot Password" on the sign-in page, which opens a modal prompting them to enter their registered email address. After submitting the email, users receive a password reset link via email. Clicking the link redirects them to a secure password reset page where they can enter and confirm a new password. Upon successful password reset, users are redirected to the sign-in page to log in with their new credentials.

### Figure X. Demo Session Feature
The demo session feature was added to address the high demand from learners and limited availability of approved tutors during initial platform deployment. This feature allows learners to experience the full functionality of the video session interface, including peer-to-peer video communication, interactive whiteboard, real-time chat, and resource sharing, without requiring an actual tutor. The demo session provides a simulated tutoring environment where learners can test features, familiarize themselves with the interface, and build confidence before booking real sessions. This feature helps onboard new users and reduces anxiety about using the platform's video collaboration tools.

### Figure X. Platform Announcements (Posted Announcements)
The announcements page displays all messages published by administrators, organized chronologically with the most recent announcements appearing first. Each announcement includes a title, content (with markdown formatting support), publication date, and the administrator who created it. Learners and tutors can access this page from their respective dashboards to stay informed about platform updates, policy changes, maintenance schedules, new features, and other important information. The announcements remain accessible until administrators choose to delete them, creating a persistent record of platform communications.

### Figure X. Email Verification Process
The email verification process ensures account security and validates user email addresses. After registration, users receive a verification email containing a confirmation link. Upon clicking the link, users are redirected to their role-specific dashboard, and the verification page displays a confirmation message ("All set, you can close this tab") indicating successful verification. The "Check your email" page transitions to the confirmation state once verification is complete. This process prevents unauthorized account creation and ensures users have access to a valid email address for platform communications.
