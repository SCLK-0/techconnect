## Tips for Presentation

1. **Be confident** - You built something impressive
2. **Show, don't just tell** - Live demo is crucial
3. **Anticipate questions** - Have answers ready
4. **Be honest** - If something isn't done, explain the timeline
5. **Focus on value** - Emphasize how it solves their problems
6. **Have backup** - Screenshots in case of technical issues
7. **Take notes** - Write down all feedback and requests
8. **Set expectations** - Be clear about timelines and costs

Good luck with your presentation!
