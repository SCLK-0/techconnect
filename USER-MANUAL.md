## Introduction

Welcome to **TechConnect** - your online tutoring platform that connects learners with expert tutors through live video sessions.

### What is TechConnect?

TechConnect is a comprehensive tutoring platform that enables:
- **Learners** to find and connect with qualified tutors
- **Tutors** to offer their expertise and manage sessions
- **Administrators** to oversee the platform and ensure quality

### Key Features

- 🎥 Live video sessions with HD quality
- 📝 Interactive whiteboard for real-time collaboration
- 💬 In-session chat messaging
- 📁 File sharing capabilities
- 📅 Flexible scheduling system
- ⚡ Instant session requests
- ⭐ Rating and feedback system
- 📊 Progress tracking

## Getting Started

### System Requirements

**Minimum Requirements:**
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Stable internet connection (5 Mbps or higher)
- Webcam and microphone (for video sessions)
- Speakers or headphones

**Recommended:**
- 10 Mbps or higher internet speed
- HD webcam
- Noise-canceling headphones with microphone

### Creating an Account

#### Step 1: Visit the Platform

1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to: `https://techconnect-sand.vercel.app`
3. Click on **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**

**[INSERT IMAGE: Landing page with Sign Up button highlighted]**  
*Image location: `docs/images/landing-page.png`*

#### Step 2: Choose Your Role

Select your role:
- **Learner** - If you want to learn from tutors
- **Tutor** - If you want to teach and offer tutoring services
- **Admin** - For platform administrators (requires special access)

**[INSERT IMAGE: Role selection screen]**  
*Image location: `docs/images/role-selection.png`*

#### Step 3: Complete Registration

1. Enter your email address
2. Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
3. Fill in your personal information:
   - Full name
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Profile picture (optional)
4. Click **"Create Account"**

**[INSERT IMAGE: Registration form]**  
*Image location: `docs/images/registration-form.png`*

#### Step 4: Verify Your Email

1. Check your email inbox
2. Open the verification email from TechConnect
3. Click the verification link
4. You'll be redirected to the login page

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Cannot Log In

**Problem:** "Invalid email or password" error

**Solutions:**
1. Check your email is spelled correctly
2. Ensure Caps Lock is off
3. Click "Forgot Password" to reset
4. Clear browser cache and try again

---

#### Camera Not Working

**Problem:** Video feed shows black screen or "No camera detected"

**Solutions:**
1. Check camera is connected
2. Allow browser permission to access camera
3. Close other apps using the camera
4. Try a different browser
5. Restart your computer

**[INSERT IMAGE: Browser camera permission prompt]**  
*Image location: `docs/images/camera-permission.png`*

---

#### Microphone Not Working

**Problem:** Other participant can't hear you

**Solutions:**
1. Check microphone is connected
2. Allow browser permission to access microphone
3. Check microphone is not muted in system settings
4. Test microphone in device settings
5. Try a different microphone

---

#### Poor Video Quality

**Problem:** Video is pixelated or laggy

**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet speed (minimum 5 Mbps)
2. Close other bandwidth-heavy applications
3. Move closer to WiFi router
4. Turn off video and use audio only
5. Reduce video quality in settings

---

#### Whiteboard Not Syncing

**Problem:** Changes on whiteboard not visible to other participant

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Check internet connection
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try a different browser
5. Report issue to admin

---

#### Cannot Join Session

**Problem:** "Session not found" or "Unable to join" error

**Solutions:**
1. Check session time is correct
2. Refresh the page
3. Log out and log back in
4. Check your internet connection
5. Contact support if issue persists

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is TechConnect free to use?**  
A: Registration is free. Tutors set their own rates, and learners pay per session.

**Q: How do I change my password?**  
A: Go to Settings → Security → Change Password

**Q: Can I use TechConnect on mobile?**  
A: Yes, TechConnect is mobile-responsive and works on phones and tablets.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**  
A: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account (this action is permanent)

---

### For Learners

**Q: How do I find a good tutor?**  
A: Check tutor ratings, reviews, and qualifications. Book a trial session to see if they're a good fit.

**Q: Can I cancel a session?**  
A: Yes, but cancellation policies may apply. Cancel at least 24 hours in advance when possible.

**Q: What if my tutor doesn't show up?**  
A: Contact support immediately. You'll receive a full refund.

**Q: Can I request a specific tutor?**  
A: Yes, you can book directly with any available tutor.

---

### For Tutors

**Q: How do I get approved as a tutor?**  
A: Complete your profile with qualifications and experience. Admin will review within 2-3 business days.

**Q: How do I get paid?**  
A: Currently through the donation system. Payment integration coming soon.

**Q: Can I set my own rates?**  
A: Yes, you set your hourly rate in your profile settings.

**Q: What if a student is disruptive?**  
A: You can end the session and report the issue to admin.

---

### Technical Questions

**Q: What internet speed do I need?**  
A: Minimum 5 Mbps, recommended 10 Mbps or higher.

**Q: Do I need to download any software?**  
A: No, TechConnect works entirely in your web browser.

**Q: Are sessions recorded?**  
A: Only if both participants agree. Recordings are stored securely.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: Yes, all data is encrypted and we follow industry security standards.
