## Introduction

Welcome to **TechConnect** - A peer tutoring platform designed specifically for the College of Industrial Technology at Southern Luzon State University. Connect with fellow students for academic support through live video sessions.

### What is TechConnect?

TechConnect is a peer-to-peer tutoring platform that enables:
- **Learners** to find and connect with verified student tutors
- **Tutors** to share their expertise and help fellow students

## Getting Started

### System Requirements

**Minimum Requirements:**
- Modern web browser (Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+)
- Stable internet connection (5 Mbps or higher recommended)
- Webcam and microphone (for video sessions)
- Speakers or headphones

**Not Supported:**
- Internet Explorer
- Older browser versions


### Creating an Account

#### Step 1: Visit the Platform

1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to: `https://cit-techconnect.org`
3. Click on **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**

#### Step 2: Choose Your Role

Select your role:
- **Learner** - If you want to learn from peer tutors
- **Tutor** - If you want to teach and offer tutoring services (requires verification)

#### Step 3: Complete Registration

1. Enter your CIT email address
2. Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
3. Fill in your personal information:
   - Full name
   - Course
   - Year level
   - Profile picture (optional)
   - Bio (optional)
4. Click **"Create Account"**

#### Step 4: Verify Your Email

1. Check your email inbox
2. Open the verification email from TechConnect
3. Click the verification link
4. You'll be redirected to the login page

**Note for Tutors:** After email verification, your tutor application will be reviewed by administrators. You'll receive an email notification once approved or if additional information is needed.

### Logging In

1. Go to the TechConnect homepage
2. Click **"Login"**
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click **"Sign In"**

## For Learners

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in as a learner, you'll see your dashboard with:
- Upcoming sessions
- Recent tutors
- Quick actions (Find Tutors, Request Session)
- Session history
- Announcements and events


### Finding a Tutor

#### Browse Tutors

1. Click **"Find Tutors"** in the sidebar
2. Browse through available verified tutors
3. View tutor profiles including:
   - Subjects they teach
   - Year level and course
   - Average rating and review count
   - Online status (green = available for instant sessions)
   - Bio and expertise

#### Search for Tutors

Use the search bar with fuzzy search:
- Type tutor name or subject
- System finds matches even with typos
- Results ranked by relevance

#### Filter Tutors

Use filters to narrow your search:
- **Subject** - Select specific subjects (Programming, Electronics, etc.)
- **Year Level** - Filter by tutor's year level
- **Online Status** - Show only tutors currently online
- **Rating** - Filter by minimum rating (e.g., 4+ stars)
- **Booked Tutors** - Show only tutors you've previously booked

#### Favorite Tutors

- Click the heart icon on a tutor's profile to add them to favorites
- Access your favorite tutors quickly from "My Favorites"

### Booking a Session

#### Scheduled Session

1. Click on a tutor's profile
2. Click **"Book Session"**
3. Select a date and time from the tutor's available slots
4. Add session details:
   - Subject/topic
   - Description of what you need help with
   - Preferred duration
5. Click **"Confirm Booking"**
6. Wait for tutor to accept (you'll receive email and in-app notification)


#### Instant Session

For immediate help when tutors are online:

1. Find a tutor with green "Online" badge
2. Click **"Request Instant Session"**
3. Select the subject
4. Describe what you need help with
5. Click **"Send Request"**
6. Tutor can accept or decline in real-time
7. If accepted, session starts immediately

**Note:** Instant sessions are subject to tutor availability.

### Managing Your Sessions

#### View Sessions

1. Go to **"My Sessions"** in the sidebar
2. See tabs for:
   - **Upcoming** - Scheduled and pending sessions
   - **Completed** - Past sessions with feedback
   - **Cancelled** - Cancelled sessions with reasons

#### Cancel or Reschedule a Session

1. Go to **"My Sessions"**
2. Find the session
3. Click **"Cancel"** or **"Reschedule"**
4. Provide a reason (optional but recommended)
5. Confirm action

**Cancellation Policy:** Try to cancel at least 24 hours in advance when possible.

### Joining a Session

#### Before Session Starts

- You'll receive email and in-app notifications
- Join button becomes active 5 minutes before scheduled time

#### Joining the Session

1. Go to **"My Sessions"**
2. Click **"Join Session"** button (green)
3. Allow browser permissions for camera and microphone
4. Test your devices
5. Click **"Enter Session"**
6. Wait in the waiting room for tutor to admit you
